About Cloud Summit

Canada's Largest Multi-Cloud Conference

Our History

Cloud Summit began with a vision to create Canada's premier multi-cloud conference, bringing together professionals from across the cloud ecosystem. What started as a single-city event has grown into a nationwide movement, connecting thousands of cloud practitioners, decision-makers, and innovators.

Through the years, we've maintained our commitment to excellence, community, and charitable giving. Each Cloud Summit event not only advances cloud knowledge and networking but also contributes to meaningful causes, raising over $10,000 annually for local charities.

Today, Cloud Summit proudly serves both Vancouver and Toronto, establishing itself as the largest gathering of AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and IBM Cloud experts in Canada. Our events feature world-class speakers, hands-on workshops, and unparalleled networking opportunities that continue to shape the future of cloud computing in Canada.

Canadian Public Cloud Association's Purpose

  1. To educate the local technology community.
  2. To give back to the local community through charity.
  3. To bring together the local technology community.
