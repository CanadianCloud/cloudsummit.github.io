What's Included

Booths

All booths include a table where sponsors can decorate, run competitions, and provide swag. Higher tier sponsors receive premium placement and interactive booths with power and internet access.

Registration & Swag

Attendees receive branded swag bags and event badges at registration, giving sponsors the ability to scan badges for follow-up. Lego swag featuring sponsor logos is also included.

Stages

Vancouver features a main stage plus multiple smaller stages. Toronto has a dedicated main stage for keynotes and talks. All main stage content is professionally recorded and uploaded to our channel.

E-Sports Hackathon

Partnered with HackerRivals.com, we transform hackathons into entertaining sporting events with audience engagement. Sponsors' solutions and messaging are integrated into the event experience.

Workshops

Private hands-on or guided training sessions with everything provided: registration, tables, chairs, screens, microphones, and a private stage for your presentations.

Digital Signage

Digital displays throughout the venue showcase event maps, schedules, sponsor logos, and advertising videos for maximum brand visibility.
